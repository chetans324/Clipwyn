Neural virality index

Two models judge every moment: one reads only what was said, the other sees only how the room reacted. A clip is made when both agree, and scored out of a hundred before you ever see it.

FAQ

Got any questions left?

The things people ask before they run their first stream through Clipwyn.

Who can use Clipwyn?

Access is by approval. Request an account, the Braivex team reviews it, and an email tells you the moment it is switched on. That is what keeps the studio private and the compute for members.

Do I need an AI key?

No. The AI runs on Braivex's servers and is part of the studio, so there is nothing to install, no key to paste and no AI bill on your side.

What if the AI is unsure about a moment?

Clipwyn still makes clips, picking the loudest moments that have speech in them, so a quiet AI day never stops the queue. The AI is what writes the hooks, titles and scores.

Which videos work best?

Anything with people talking and reacting: live streams, gaming VODs, podcasts, interviews. Paste a YouTube, Twitch or Kick link, or upload a video file. Captions are written from English speech.

How long are the clips, and what shape?

Vertical 9:16, the shape Shorts, Reels and TikTok use. They run 15 to 60 seconds by default and you can set your own range. Choose a blurred fill, a zoomed frame or a centre crop.

Can I change a clip afterwards?

Yes. Open any clip to trim the start and end, rewrite the hook and title, or switch caption style and layout, then re-make it.

Who can see my videos?

Only you. Creators, videos, clips and connected accounts are yours alone; Braivex admins see how much AI a studio uses, never its files.

Can I clip any streamer?

Clipwyn is a tool. You are responsible for having the right to clip and post a creator's content; most clipping programs ask you to follow the creator's own rules.
